Case study

Oskar

Helped Oskar gain 500+ karma himself

Context / Goal

Oskar was a solo founder new to Reddit. He wanted to understand the platform and participate in a way that felt honest and safe.

Challenge

He did not know subreddit culture or rules, and was nervous about posting. The goal was not to do everything for him, but to help him do it himself.

What I did

  • Explained subreddit culture, rules, and how karma actually works.
  • Found relevant conversations together.
  • Coached him on writing helpful replies instead of promotional ones.
  • Reviewed what worked so he could continue independently.

Results

Oskar gained 500+ karma himself and learned how to take part on Reddit on his own. His account now brings 400+ weekly visitors consistently.

Evidence

Oskar's own Reddit activity is the evidence — the karma and traffic came from posts and comments he published himself.

"I was terrified of posting on Reddit. Ravikesh helped me understand the culture and find conversations where I could actually contribute. I gained 500+ karma myself and learned how to keep going on my own."

Learnings

Teaching the founder creates longer-term value than doing everything for them.
