Case study

Vilva.ai

300+ users from Reddit in 1 month

Context / Goal

Vilva.ai wanted early users from Reddit without spam, paid promotion, or risking the domain in communities that dislike self-promotion.

Challenge

The product was new, with almost no presence on Reddit. The goal was to find the right communities and earn trust before asking for attention.

What I did

  • Identified relevant subreddits and read their rules.
  • Warmed up accounts by helping people with real questions.
  • Shared the product only where it genuinely fit the discussion.
  • Tracked which conversations brought signups.

Results

300+ users from Reddit in 1 month.

LinkedIn followers doubled from 300 to 600, and X impressions crossed 10,000 in the first month. Platforms managed: Reddit, LinkedIn, and X.
