Case study

Creator Economy Startup (Stealth)

30+ beta creators recruited

Context / Goal

The product stayed private while the team looked for early creators to test with. Recruited creators reportedly had audiences in the 10K–100K range.

Challenge

No public product name, no domain to link, and no audience. The work had to stay inside genuine conversations about creator pain points.

What I did

  • Mapped conversations where creators discussed real workflow problems.
  • Joined with useful answers instead of product pitches.
  • Invited interested creators to a private beta by direct message.
  • Kept the product name and domain out of public threads.

Results

30+ beta creators recruited. Recruited creators reportedly had audiences in the 10K–100K range.
