Case study

CreatorsThatSpark

70+ members in 5 days

Context / Goal

Build a creator community from scratch. Other reported results: 500+ launch-week views and 200+ weekly visitors.

Challenge

No existing audience and no ad spend. The community had to earn attention through a clear purpose and useful discussions.

What I did

  • Defined a clear purpose for the community.
  • Seeded discussions that creators actually wanted to join.
  • Invited relevant creators and set basic moderation norms.
  • Kept the focus on useful conversation, not self-promotion.

Results

70+ members in 5 days, 500+ launch-week views, and 200+ weekly visitors.
